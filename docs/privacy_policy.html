Privacy Policy — “Koora Trivia”

Developer: Oasis Forge · Effective date: 8 September 2026 · Contact: thepromptkitchen@gmail.com

Oasis Forge respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the “Koora Trivia” app handles and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to the following.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app requires no sign-in and no account, and it sends no information about you to our servers — because we run no server. All of your game progress (points, stars, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device only, and is permanently erased when the app is removed or its data is cleared.

2. Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect data such as your Advertising ID and information about your device and ad interactions. This data is governed by Google’s privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses partner data

On first launch we show a consent dialog (via Google’s UMP platform) so you can set your preferences for personalized ads; you can review these later from the app or your device settings.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: only to remind you of the daily challenge. You can turn them off in settings.
  • Internet access: only to load ads; the game content works offline.

4. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add new features (such as cloud save in the future). Any change will appear on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact

For any privacy question, contact us at thepromptkitchen@gmail.com.
