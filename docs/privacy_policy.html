Privacy Policy — “Koora Trivia”

Developer: Oasis Forge · Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Contact: oasisforge.support@gmail.com

Oasis Forge respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the “Koora Trivia” app handles and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to the following.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app requires no sign-in and no account, and it sends no information about you to our servers — because we run no server. All of your game progress (points, stars, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device, and is erased when the app is removed or its data is cleared.

Android backup: if backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app’s saved progress in your device backup, which Google stores and which you control from your device settings. We cannot access that backup.

Messages you send us: the app can prepare an email to us, to send feedback or to report a mistake in a question. Nothing is sent automatically: the draft opens in your own email app, where you can see and edit everything in it — including the app version and the recent error messages kept on your device — and it is sent only if you send it. We use what you send only to reply to you and to fix problems.

2. Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • your Advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • your interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and how often the app freezes.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is governed by Google’s privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses partner data

3. Your ad choices

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google’s consent form and waits for your choice before requesting any ad. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options; this option appears wherever consent is required. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s settings. The same Settings section links to this policy.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: only to remind you of the daily challenge. You can turn them off in settings.
  • Internet access: to load ads and show Google’s consent form.

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add new features (such as cloud save in the future). Any change will appear on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

For any privacy question, contact us at oasisforge.support@gmail.com.
